A week and a half of lunches from a single oven session. The rule that shapes the plan: chicken only stays in the fridge for three days, so days 1–3 are chicken, day 4 is tofu, and everything else goes in the freezer.

## Packing

Split each component into equal piles first, then assemble:

- **Rice:** 3/4 cup per dabba, a mix of brown and basmati
- **Protein:** 6 equal piles of chicken, 5 of tofu
- **Veg + chickpeas:** about 1 cup (roughly a third to half of it chickpeas)
- **Sauce:** in a small separate container. Sour cream for the three fridge chicken bowls; tahini for everything else.

Cool everything fully before lidding, label each dabba with the day you'll eat it, and freeze them flat.

## Moving meals from freezer to fridge

Each evening, move the next day's dabba from the freezer to the fridge. From day 5 on, that's a nightly 10-second habit.

## Reheating

Microwave covered for 3–4 minutes, stirring halfway, with a splash of water if the rice looks dry. Chicken should reach 165°F. Add the sauce and a fresh salad (lettuce, tomato, onion, lemon, olive oil, salt) after heating. Reheat once only.
