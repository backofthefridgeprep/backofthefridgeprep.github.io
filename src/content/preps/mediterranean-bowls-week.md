A week and a half of lunches from one oven session. Chicken only keeps three days in the fridge, so that's what goes in first. Everything else freezes.

## Reheating

Microwave covered for 3–4 minutes, stirring halfway, with a splash of water if the rice looks dry. Chicken should reach 165°F. Add the sauce and a fresh salad after heating.
