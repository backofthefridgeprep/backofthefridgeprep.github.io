The trick is to cook parts, not finished plates. Sauces, roasted veg, tofu and rice keep well. Noodles, pasta, avocado and fresh herbs get done on the night. Eat the dishes with the shortest fridge life first.

## Storing and reheating

| Part | Fridge | Freezer | Reheat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coconut broth | 4 days | 3 months | Low simmer; whisk if it splits |
| Crispy chili tofu | 4 days | No | Air fryer or pan, 5 min |
| Shiitake + broccolini or broccoli | 3 days | No | Quick pan toss |
| Sofritas | 4 days | 3 months | Microwave or pan |
| Fajita veg | 4 days | 2 months | Microwave or pan |
| Black beans | 4 days | 3 months | Microwave, splash of water |
| Cilantro-lime rice | 4 days | 2 months | Microwave covered, sprinkle of water |
| Pico | 2 days | No | Cold |
| Guac | 1 day, plastic pressed on | No | Cold |
| Roasted cauliflower + chickpeas | 4 days | No | 400°F oven or air fryer, 6–8 min |
| Pickled onions | 2–3 weeks | No | Cold |
| Taco dressing / crema | 5 days | No | Cold |
| Tofu cream | 3 days | No | Straight into the pan |
| Seared cremini | 4 days | No | Straight into the pan |
