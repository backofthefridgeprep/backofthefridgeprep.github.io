Twenty containers sounds like a lot. It's four dishes, and three of them cook at the same time on different appliances.

## Equipment

9x13 baking dish and foil, a 6-quart Instant Pot, a large skillet or wok, a medium pot for rice, a large pot for blanching, a blender, a bowl of ice water, and about 20 containers with labels.

## For little ones

Before the salt and soy go in, set aside a few plain paneer cubes, soft tofu strips, halved or smashed edamame, soft potato and zucchini from the soup, and small broccoli florets.
