1. Add the oats, milk and water to a saucepan.
2. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat.
3. Cook 5–7 minutes, stirring often, until soft and thick.
4. Turn the heat to low.
5. Stir one ladle of hot oats into the beaten eggs. This warms them so they don't scramble.
6. Pour the egg mixture into the pot.
7. Stir constantly for 2–3 minutes, until no runny egg is left. Egg must be fully cooked for babies.
8. Turn off the heat. Stir in the ghee, and cinnamon or elaichi if using.
9. Cool quickly and split into 4 portions. 2 in the fridge, 2 in the freezer.
10. When serving, stir in a thawed fruit purée cube.
