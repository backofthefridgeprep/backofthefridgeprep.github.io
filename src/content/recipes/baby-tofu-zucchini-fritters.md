1. Wrap the tofu in a clean towel. Set something heavy on top for 10–15 minutes.
2. Crumble the tofu finely.
3. Grate the zucchini.
4. Wrap it in a clean towel and squeeze out as much water as you can.
5. Sauté the pepper in a little ghee for 2 minutes until soft. Let it cool a bit.
6. Mix the tofu, zucchini, pepper, cheese, spices and egg yolk (if using) in a bowl.
7. Add the flour a spoonful at a time until the mix is thick and holds its shape.
8. Shape into small flat patties.
9. Cook in ghee over low-medium heat, about 3 minutes per side, until golden and firm.
10. Cool on a rack or plate, 20–30 minutes.
11. Freeze on a parchment-lined tray, not touching, 1–2 hours. Then bag.
12. Serve 2–3 with dahi.

Falling apart? The mix is too wet. Squeeze the zucchini again, or add another spoonful of flour.
