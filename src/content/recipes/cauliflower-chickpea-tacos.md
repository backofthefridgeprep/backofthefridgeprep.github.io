1. Make the onions first. Whisk the vinegar, hot water, sugar and salt.
2. Pour it over the onion in a jar. Ready in 30 minutes.
3. Heat the oven to 425°F. Line a sheet pan.
4. Toss the cauliflower and chickpeas with the oil, hot sauce, lime and seasoning on the pan.
5. Spread them out. Don't crowd the pan.
6. Roast 25–30 minutes, flipping once, until the edges char.
7. Whisk the dressing. Put it in a squeeze bottle, or a bag with the corner snipped. Thin with water until it drizzles.
8. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan, 20–30 seconds per side.
9. Fill with lettuce, cauliflower and chickpeas, pickled onions and avocado.
10. Add a zigzag of dressing and some cilantro.
