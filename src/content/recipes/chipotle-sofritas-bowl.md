## Oven

1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Line two sheet pans.
2. Blend the chipotles, adobo, tomato paste, garlic, spices, soy, lime, oil and salt into a paste.
3. Toss the paste with the crumbled tofu. Spread it on one pan.
4. Toss the peppers and onion with the oil and spices on the second pan.
5. Bake both 25–30 minutes.
6. At 15 minutes, stir the sofritas and swap the racks.
7. The sofritas are done when the edges are browned and chewy.

## Rice

1. Bring the rice, water, salt, oil and bay leaf to a boil.
2. Cover and simmer on low for 12 minutes.
3. Rest 10 minutes off the heat.
4. Fluff with the lime juice, zest and cilantro.

## Beans

1. Simmer the beans with their liquid and the spices for 10 minutes, until saucy.
2. Finish with a squeeze of lime.

## Guac and pico

1. Mash the avocados with lime and salt.
2. Fold in the onion, jalapeño and cilantro.
3. Mix all the pico ingredients. Let it sit 10 minutes.

## Build

1. Rice, beans, sofritas and fajita veg first.
2. Then lettuce, corn, pico, guac, cheese, crema and hot sauce.
3. For meal prep, pack rice, beans, sofritas and veg together (hot side). Pack lettuce, pico, cheese and crema separately (cold side).
4. Make guac on the day, or press plastic wrap right onto it and eat within a day.
