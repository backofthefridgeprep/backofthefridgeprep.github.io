1. Heat the ghee in the pressure cooker, or the Instant Pot on sauté.
2. Add the jeera. Let it sizzle.
3. Add the hing and haldi. Stir a few seconds.
4. Add the dal, rice, all the vegetables, peas, spinach and water. Stir well.
5. Pressure cook 4–5 whistles, or 12 minutes on high in the Instant Pot.
6. Let the pressure release naturally.
7. Mash with a potato masher, or pulse briefly with a hand blender. Leave a few small soft lumps.
8. If it's too thick, stir in a little hot water.
9. Cool quickly. Keep 2 portions in the fridge and freeze the rest.
