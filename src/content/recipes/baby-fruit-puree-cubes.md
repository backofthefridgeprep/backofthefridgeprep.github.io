## Pear

1. Steam the pear for 5–8 minutes until very soft.
2. Blend until smooth.

## Mango

1. If the mango is ripe, blend it raw until smooth. No cooking needed.

## Prune

1. Soak the prunes in hot water for 10 minutes.
2. Blend with a little of the soaking water.

## Freeze

1. Spoon each purée into silicone moulds and cover.
2. Freeze until solid, 6–12 hours.
3. Pop the cubes into zip-lock bags. Label with fruit and date.
4. The night before, move one cube to the fridge to thaw.

Prune is gentle on the tummy. Start with half a cube.
