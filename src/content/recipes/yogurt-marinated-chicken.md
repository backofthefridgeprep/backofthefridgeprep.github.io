## Thaw safely (cold-water method)

1. Check the packaging for tears, then keep it sealed.
2. Submerge fully in cold tap water. Not warm: the surface would warm up before the inside thaws.
3. Change the water every 30 minutes. Thighs thaw in about 1–1.5 hours.
4. USDA guidance is to cook right after cold-water thawing. Going straight into the marinade and into the fridge overnight (never the counter) is the closest safe version of that.

## Marinate and sear

1. Mix the yogurt, ginger-garlic, lemon, paprika or chili powder and cumin. Coat the chicken and refrigerate overnight.
2. Next morning, sear for about 10 minutes.
