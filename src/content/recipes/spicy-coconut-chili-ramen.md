## Crispy tofu

1. Tear the pressed tofu by hand into bite-size pieces, so the edges are rough.
2. Toss with the cornstarch and salt.
3. Heat the oil in a nonstick pan over medium-high.
4. Fry the tofu 8–10 minutes, turning, until crisp on all sides.
5. Turn the heat to low. Add the doubanjiang, chili powder, hot sauce, soy, sugar and 1 tbsp water.
6. Toss 1 minute until coated. Set aside.

## Shiitake and broccolini (or broccoli)

1. Wipe the pan. Heat both oils on high.
2. Sear the shiitake 4–5 minutes, stirring little, until browned.
3. Add the broccolini (or broccoli) and garlic. Cook 2–3 minutes.
4. Add the light and dark soy. Toss 1 minute. It should stay bright and crisp-tender.

## Coconut broth

1. Warm the sesame oil in a pot over medium-low.
2. Cook the garlic (and ginger) 1 minute. Don't let it brown.
3. Add the broth, coconut milk, soy and sugar.
4. Simmer gently 10 minutes. Don't boil hard, or the coconut milk splits.
5. Add the lime juice. Taste for salt.

## Serve

1. Cook the noodles as the package says. Drain and rinse briefly.
2. Put noodles in each bowl and ladle the broth over.
3. Add tofu, mushrooms and broccolini side by side.
4. Top with green onion and chili crisp.
5. For meal prep, keep broth, tofu, veg and noodles in separate containers. Cook noodles fresh, or toss cooked noodles in a little sesame oil (keeps 2 days).
