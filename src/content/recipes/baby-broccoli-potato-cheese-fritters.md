1. Steam the broccoli 5–6 minutes until very tender.
2. Chop it finely.
3. Mix the mashed potato, broccoli and cheese in a bowl.
4. Add the flour a spoonful at a time until the mix holds its shape.
5. Shape into small flat patties, about 2 inches wide.
6. Heat a little ghee in a pan over low-medium heat.
7. Cook 2–3 minutes per side until golden.
8. Cool on a rack or plate, 20–30 minutes.
9. Freeze on a parchment-lined tray, not touching, 1–2 hours. Then bag.
10. Serve 2–3 with dahi. If baby won't eat them whole, mash 2 into dahi with a little ghee.
