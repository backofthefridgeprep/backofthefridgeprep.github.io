1. Blend the tofu and half-and-half until completely smooth, about 1 minute.
2. Boil the rigatoni in salted water, 1 minute short of the package time.
3. Save 1 cup of pasta water. Drain.
4. Heat the oil in a wide pan over high.
5. Add the mushrooms in one layer (two batches if needed). Leave them 3–4 minutes before stirring.
6. Cook 8–10 minutes total until golden. Salt them only at the end.
7. Lower to medium. Add the butter and onion. Cook 3 minutes.
8. Add the garlic, tomato paste, paprika, Italian seasoning and pepper flakes. Cook 1 minute.
9. Pour in the tofu cream. Simmer gently 2–3 minutes.
10. Stir in the spinach until wilted.
11. Add the pasta and cheese. Toss with pasta water until the sauce coats every piece.
12. Season with salt and pepper. Top with more cheese.
