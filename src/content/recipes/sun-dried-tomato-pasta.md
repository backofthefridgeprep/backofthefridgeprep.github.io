1. Toast the almonds in a dry pan over medium heat, 3–4 minutes, shaking often, until golden. Tip onto a plate.
2. Boil the spaghetti in well-salted water until just al dente.
3. Save 1 cup of pasta water. Drain.
4. Meanwhile, heat the olive oil and sun-dried tomato oil in a wide pan over medium-high.
5. Add the cherry tomatoes. Let them blister 5–6 minutes, until a few burst.
6. Lower to medium. Add the garlic, sun-dried tomatoes, pepper flakes, salt and sugar. Cook 2 minutes.
7. Take the pan off the heat.
8. Whisk the sour cream with 1/4 cup pasta water. Stir it in. This keeps it from splitting.
9. Add the spaghetti and Parmesan. Toss over low heat.
10. Add pasta water a splash at a time, until glossy.
11. Top with basil, almonds and more Parmesan.
12. To prep ahead, make only the tomato base (steps 4–6) and keep it up to 4 days. On the night, reheat it, add the sour cream off the heat, and toss with fresh pasta.
