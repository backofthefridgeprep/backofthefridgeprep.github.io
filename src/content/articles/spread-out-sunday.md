The first few times I did a Sunday prep, I tried to do it all in one go on Sunday evening. It doesn't work. By the third dish you're tired, the kitchen is a mess, and dinner is late. Here's what works better for me.

## Shop on Friday night, if you can

If the groceries are already in the fridge on Saturday morning, Sunday is only about cooking. Friday night stores are quieter, too. If Friday isn't possible, anything before Sunday helps.

## Cut and prep on Sunday

Washing, chopping, peeling, soaking, marinating: this is most of the work, and it doesn't need the stove. I do it earlier in the day, in between everything else, so by the time I start cooking everything is sitting in bowls, one bowl per dish.

## Cook through the day, not in one push

Instead of one long evening, cook in stretches. Rice and a pot of soup in the late morning. One oven dish after lunch. The rest in the afternoon. Each stretch is short, and nothing piles up at once.

## The short version

- Friday: shop.
- Sunday, early: cut and prep.
- Sunday, through the day: cook in short stretches.
- Sunday evening: pack, label, done.
